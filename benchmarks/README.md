## Always included

| Item | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery and scoping | 8% of build hours, at least 3 |
| Testing and QA | 12% of build hours, at least 4 |
| Deployment and handoff | 4 |

## Formula

1. Build hours = sum of the deliverables above (× 0.85 or × 1.25 when the brief says so).
2. Subtotal = discovery + build + testing + deployment.
3. Total hours = subtotal × (1 + buffer), with a buffer of 15, 20 or 25%.
4. Fixed price = total hours × hourly rate.

## Scope protection clauses

- **Scope:** only the deliverables listed are included. Anything else is a change request.
- **Change requests:** each one gets a written estimate (hours × rate) and starts after written approval.
- **Revisions:** [2] rounds per deliverable; more are billed hourly.
- **Client inputs:** delays in content or access move the timeline by the same number of days.
- **Ownership:** rights transfer on full payment.
